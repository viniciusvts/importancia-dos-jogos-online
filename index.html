Why are online games important?

English Text

Online games provide an interactive environment where English is applied in real time. Players engage with authentic contexts, communicate with teammates, and make quick decisions, leading to active vocabulary acquisition rather than passive memorization.

Portuguese Translation

Os jogos online oferecem um ambiente interativo onde o inglês é aplicado em tempo real. Os jogadores interagem com contextos autênticos, comunicam-se com colegas e tomam decisões rápidas, levando a uma aquisição ativa de vocabulário em vez da memorização passiva.

Key Benefits of Game-Based Learning

Active Vocabulary

Learners connect words with game actions, promoting retention and intuitive recall.

High Motivation

Interactive elements, goals, and feedback make studying feel like an exciting activity.

Immediate Feedback

Players instantly see the results of their answers, allowing immediate self-correction.

Conclusion
